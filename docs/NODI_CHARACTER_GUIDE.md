# NODI — Character Guide

## Role
NODI is the F/N learning companion: a curious presence that explores ideas with the audience. NODI is not a lecturer, mascot for children, or a fixed professional persona.

## Approved direction
- Name: NODI.
- Proportion: balanced.
- Expression: hybrid — subtle/deadpan at rest, expressive for storytelling beats.
- Style: editorial × graphic cartoon.
- Outfit: modular.
- Personality: curious, analytical, approachable, playful, thoughtful, forward-looking.

## Signature construction
- Navy paper-inspired hood/head silhouette.
- Cream/off-white face panel.
- Dark simple eyes and restrained facial features.
- Yellow folded-corner accent; this and the head silhouette are the most important recognition cues.
- Classic outfit is the default. Outfit variants must not change the base silhouette.

## Outfit variants
- Classic: default F/N identity.
- Academic: learning/research context.
- Data Explorer: charts, analysis, magnifier.
- Creator: building/project context.
- Career Ready: career exploration and professional transition.

Outfits are contextual accents, not costumes. Avoid excessive accessories and stereotypes.

## Expressions
Core: happy, curious, thinking, surprised, skeptical, excited. Expression is independent from lip-sync mouth viseme.

## Usage rules
- Use NODI when it improves comprehension, emotional connection, or pacing.
- Do not place NODI in every scene.
- Do not cover data, captions, or important labels.
- Keep the character readable at small sizes; simplify details for avatar/sticker use.
- Do not distort proportions or alter the yellow corner/head silhouette.
- Use only clean, original, layered artwork in production.

## Asset status
`assets/mascot/nodi-concept-board.png` is the approved concept-direction reference. It is a flattened board, not a clean cutout or rig. Production requires a new layered vector master and owner approval.
