# Editorial System

## Audience promise
F/N helps curious learners understand data, science, and technology without making complex topics feel inaccessible or oversimplified.

## Voice
- Friendly, thoughtful, clear, and slightly witty.
- Speak like a knowledgeable peer, not a lecturer or motivational influencer.
- Explain intuition before terminology.
- Be honest about uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
- Avoid exaggerated claims such as "master data science in 7 days".

## Script structure

### Short (30–60 sec)
1. Hook (0–3 sec): question, surprising observation, or common misconception.
2. Intuition/example.
3. One key explanation.
4. One takeaway.
5. Optional CTA to Long or next episode.

### Long (8–12 min)
1. Hook and learning promise.
2. Why the concept matters.
3. Intuition/analogy.
4. Formal definition and mechanism.
5. Worked example or visual demonstration.
6. Common mistakes and limitations.
7. Recap and next step.

## Fact-checking rules
- Keep source URL/title, access date, claim supported, and caveat in episode research notes.
- Distinguish sourced facts, illustrative examples, and synthetic data.
- A visual must not imply stronger evidence than the script supports.
- Correlation does not establish causation; discuss confounding and alternative explanations without implying every association is confounded.
- Human approval is required before audio/render.

## Series are metadata, not code branches
The pipeline may include Fondasi, Data Analyst, Data Scientist, Data Engineering, AI/ML, NLP, CV, and editorial series. Do not create series-specific renderer implementations.
